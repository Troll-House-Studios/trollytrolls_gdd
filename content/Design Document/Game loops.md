## Map Loop

**Play Map**

The player will be able to find many different abilities around the map which will grant or allow them to punish opposing players.

**Draw Enhancements**

After the match has ended all players will be given 3 Enhancement Cards. Out of these cards you will have to select one card out of the 3. You will be able to re-roll 2 times throughout the game if you do not like them. The enhancements will grant varying buffs and de-buffs to each player.

**Select New Map**

After enhancements a random pool of maps will appear on screen and players will vote for the next map. Based off the votes it will add a percentage of the maps that will be selected. (i.e 3 players vote for map 1 and 1 player for map 2. Map 1 has a 75% chance of being selected and Map 2 has 25% chance).

## Checkout

**Checkout Open**
At random intervals throughout the match a random number of checkout lanes will open (this will remain undisclosed) after a set period of time which will be displayed to all players the checkout lanes will close.

**Drive to Checkout**
Players will have to drive to the open checkout lanes 

**Bank Items**
A checkout lane will only allow a single player to be able to bank there items. Banking items will take all of the items out of the players cart and then banking there points and crossing the items off of the list. Once a player has checked out the lane used is closed till reopening.
