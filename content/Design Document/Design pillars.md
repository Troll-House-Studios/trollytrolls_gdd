# Design pillars

**Repeatability**

The player should not just be bored. There should constantly be a difference in a games.  A players game should never be the same as the last whether its items,  maps or enhancments.

**Competition**

The players should always have a urge to beat their friends. (the player should want to no longer be friends after the game)

**Chaos**

The game needs to have many things going on at the same time players should be almost overstimulating.
