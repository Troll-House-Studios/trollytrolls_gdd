# Trolly Trolls

# Summary

**Elevator Pitch**
In a crunch of time compete with up to 4 of your friends race around varying environments in you not so standard shopping cart collecting items off of a shared list.  

**True Concept**

The game will support up to 4 players in a local split screen environment. The objective of the game is to have the most money spent on items . To spend money you must checkout with items that you and your troll brothers obtained. With that you have been provided a not so standard shopping trolly. Equipped with a motor and a grappling hook. You must navigate the differing environments to collect items off your shared list. Do whatever is necessary to checkout your items!! Customize your trolls, trolly whether it is for aesthetics or purpose any advantage is worth it.
